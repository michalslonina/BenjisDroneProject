## Sourcing Strategy

### Cheapest Option (20-30 day wait)
- **AliExpress** — Usually 30-40% cheaper than shops
- **Bulk orders** — Buy 2-3 of common parts together for shipping discount
- Order in batches to save shipping costs

### Fastest Option (1-2 weeks)
- **Local hobby shops** — Motors, batteries, propellers
- **Online EU retailers** — Faster shipping
- **Used market** — Check eBay/local classifieds for ESCs and FC

### Recommended Mix
1. **Order from AliExpress NOW:** Motors, ESCs, RF modules, connectors (long lead time = start early)
2. **Local shop:** Battery, charger, propellers (need these sooner)
3. **Have already:** ESP32 boards, filament

## Alternatives to Save Money

- **Cheaper motors:** Look for 2312/2314 KV 1900-2200 (~€4-5 each)
- **Used ESCs:** Often work fine (~€2-3 each on eBay)
- **Generic FC:** Non-name brand Betaflight-compatible (~€10-12)
- **Skip VTx initially:** Just use camera recording, add video TX later

## Total Timeline

- **Week 1-2:** Order from AliExpress + local shop
- **Week 2-4:** Components arrive (AliExpress)
- **Week 3-4:** Assembly begins with available parts
- **Week 4+:** Final assembly once all parts arrive
